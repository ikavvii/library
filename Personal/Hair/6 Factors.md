The video identifies six essential ==nutrients== for hair health and recommends the following food sources for each:

*   **Zinc:** Pumpkin seeds, cashews, almonds, curd, and beans (0:41-1:22). 
*   **Iron:** Spinach, chickpeas, kidney beans, beetroot, and pomegranate. The speaker recommends pairing these with Vitamin C sources like lemon, amla, or orange juice to aid absorption (1:22-2:15). 
*   **Omega-3 Fatty Acids:** Fish (for non-vegetarians), plus flax seeds, chia seeds, and walnuts (for vegetarians) (2:15-2:59). 
*   **Vitamin D3:** The primary source is 15-20 minutes of daily morning sunlight. Supplements may be considered after testing (2:59-3:47). 
*   **Biotin (Vitamin B7):** Eggs, peanuts, sunflower seeds, and sweet potatoes (3:47-4:30). 
*   **Protein:** Eggs, chicken, and fish for non-vegetarians; tofu, soya chunks, lentils, and Greek yogurt for vegetarians (4:30-5:20).
